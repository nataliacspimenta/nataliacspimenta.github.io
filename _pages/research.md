## Research interests

My research lies at the intersection of law and politics, with a focus on democratic erosion and public attitudes toward courts, particularly in Latin America. Methodologically, I draw mostly on experimental methods.

## Works in progress

**Defying Courts Makes Candidates Less Likely to Be Chosen: A Meta-Reanalysis of Conjoint Experiments**

This project examines whether respondents are less likely to choose court-defying candidates across 20 candidate conjoint experiments conducted in multiple countries. Using the original studies' replication files, I reconstruct direct comparisons between court-defying candidates and their non-defying opponents and estimate whether the effect of court defiance on candidate choice differs when the defying candidate shares respondents' party affiliation or policy preferences. This project was my first-year paper at Northwestern.
