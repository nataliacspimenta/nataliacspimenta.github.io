**Instructor:** Iza Ding

An undergraduate introduction to comparative politics examining how and why political systems and outcomes vary across countries. The course covers economic development, democracy and democratization, democratic backsliding, authoritarianism, state capacity, revolutions, civil society and identity, political violence, inequality, and ideology.
